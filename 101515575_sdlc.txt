DevOps in Software Development
- Continuous Integration
- Automated Testing
- Rapid Deployment
- Improved collaboration

Benefits of DevOps:
1. Faster time to market
2. Reduced deployment failures
3. Improved team collaboration
4. Faster problem resolution


Career Impact of DevOps Skills:
- High demand in job market (80% of companies hiring)
- Competitive salary ranges ($80,000-$120,000)
- Cross-functional team experience
- Foundation for cloud engineering roles
- Essential for modern software development


SDLC Integration Points:
- Planning: Infrastructure requirements
- Coding: Version control and peer reviews
- Building: Automated CI pipelines
- Testing: Automated test suites
- Deployment: CD pipelines to production
- Monitoring: Performance and usage metrics



Overall Importance: DevOps transforms traditional 
SDLC by enabling faster, more reliable software 
delivery with improved quality and collaboration.
